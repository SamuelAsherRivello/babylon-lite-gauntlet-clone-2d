# Proposal

## Why
Deliver the requested playable cooperative dungeon experience with a independently verifiable player-interface system.

## What Changes
- Hot join on page load; four always-visible class buttons and 1-4 shortcuts; WASD/arrows, aim and attack, touch movement and attack, safe blur/cancel release, local pause without freezing others, connection/full/retry UI, party roster, objectives, health and settings. Preserve template corner roles and publish verified Pages release.
