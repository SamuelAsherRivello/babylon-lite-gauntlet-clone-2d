# Proposal

## Why
Deliver the requested playable cooperative dungeon experience with a independently verifiable dungeon-combat system.

## What Changes
- One complete tile dungeon with keys, food, treasure, four destructible generators and a gated exit. Four enemy behaviors: Ghost chases, Grunt is tough, Demon fires, Sorcerer phases. Warrior, Valkyrie, Wizard and Elf have distinct attacks and speeds. Switching preserves health and cooldown. Defeat and victory lead to shared automatic replay.
