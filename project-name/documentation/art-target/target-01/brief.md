# Original dungeon sprite direction — target-01

This is generated concept imagery, not a Blender render or runtime sprite atlas. One image was generated and inspected. Eight complete separated miniatures appear in the requested order. PNG is 1536×1024 RGBA; corner and inter-row alpha samples are zero. The display preview shows colored glow around subjects; avoid carrying that glow into runtime sprites.

## Observed cues and priorities

1. Preserve silhouette before costume detail: Warrior broad axe and shoulders; Valkyrie vertical spear and round shield; Wizard wide brim plus bent cone; Elf hood and curved bow; Ghost floating tapered ragged body; Grunt squat torso and club; Demon paired horns; Sorcerer angular violet hood and orb.
2. Use large faceted forms with warm upper-left highlights and dark lower-right surfaces. Gold trim should read as a few broad bands, never fine engraved detail.
3. Keep hero families bronze/red, gold/steel-blue, navy, and forest green. Enemies use cyan, ochre, crimson and violet. These class colors are independent of persistent multiplayer identity rings/labels.
4. Model heads approximately one third of visible body height and enlarge weapon profiles. Use 6–10 sided cylinders/cones, low-subdivision spheres, simple tapered torso meshes. Hidden backs are inferred as plain capes/tunics, with no detailed unseen ornamentation.

## Limitations and simplification

The result is a strong style and silhouette reference, but its view is lower than a strict overhead camera. Render final models with a consistent elevated orthographic camera suitable for the actual game and compare silhouette at 32 and 48 px. Do not reproduce fine fingers, hair strands, belt buckles, shield stars, armor rivets or tiny teeth. Demon wings are extra concept detail and optional; horns are the required identifier. The ghost skull face is an optional stylization. Avoid attaching identity colors to classes because several players may choose the same class.

## Genuine Blender comparison criteria

No Blender output was inspected by this worker, so no 3D match is claimed. Parent should compare a genuine render against target-01: (a) eight correct identities, (b) weapon/hat/hood outlines still readable at game size, (c) consistent scale and camera, (d) transparent padding around all silhouettes, (e) warm key/cool shadow balance, (f) no glow contamination of neighboring atlas cells. Prioritize missing silhouette elements, then camera/scale, then palette; do not chase tiny decorative details.
