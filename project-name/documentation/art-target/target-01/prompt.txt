Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one concept reference sheet for original Blender-rendered 2D dungeon sprites; not final runtime assets.
Primary request: eight original low-poly fantasy miniatures in a clean 4-column by 2-row sheet, all fully framed and separated. Top row left to right: Warrior with oversized axe and bronze armor; Valkyrie with spear and round shield; Wizard with broad pointed hat and staff; Elf with curved bow and green hood. Bottom row: pale cyan floating Ghost with ragged hem; stocky ochre Grunt carrying club; crimson horned Demon; violet hooded Sorcerer with glowing orb. Each character must have a different immediately readable silhouette.
Style/medium: chunky faceted Blender miniature forms, simple geometry, no fine textures. Oversized heads and hands, clear weapons, strong silhouettes that remain legible at 32-48 pixels. Original designs, no copied game sprites.
Composition/framing: identical elevated orthographic top-down three-quarter camera for every miniature, approximately 55 degrees looking downward; characters face toward viewer, generous transparent padding, no overlap. Exactly eight subjects. No labels, typography, border, logo, UI, floor or plinth.
Lighting/mood: warm torch-like key light upper left and soft cool fill; dark ink-like shaded surfaces, restrained jewel colors, muted gold metal. Transparent background.
Constraints: all hands, weapons, hats and feet completely in frame; keep shapes simple enough to model with low-poly primitives; avoid intricate costume details, realistic faces and perspective distortion.
